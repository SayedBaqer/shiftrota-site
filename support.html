Common questions

Where is my schedule stored? On your device. Signing in is only needed for Invite & Earn.

How do I restore Pro? Settings, then ShiftRota Pro, then "Restore Purchases".

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, ShiftRota Pro.

How do I sync my shifts to my Calendar? Settings, then Calendar Sync, then turn it on and tap "Sync to Calendar now".
