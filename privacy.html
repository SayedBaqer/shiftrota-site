ShiftRota Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your schedule stays on your device

Your shift pattern, calendar overrides, notes, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to our servers.

What we collect

If you use Invite & Earn (referrals or promo codes), we collect your email address and a password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication) to provide your account, along with your referral code and redemption history. We do not collect your shift schedule, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you.

Calendar and notifications

If you turn on Calendar Sync, ShiftRota writes your shifts to a dedicated calendar on your device using Apple's EventKit — this stays on your device and in your own iCloud/Calendar account, not on our servers. Shift reminders use Apple's notification system.

Purchases

ShiftRota Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Third-party services

Google Firebase (sign-in, referrals, and announcements) and Apple (purchases, calendar, notifications). Their privacy policies apply to the data they process.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all schedule data stored on the device. If you signed in for Invite & Earn, contact us to delete your account and its associated data.

Children

ShiftRota is not directed at children under 13.
